Supported Behaviors

The model classifies every 16-frame window into one of four crowd behavior categories detected from aerial drone footage.

Normal

Calm, orderly crowd movement. Individuals walking at a typical pace without signs of distress or conflict.

Low density Steady flow No clustering
Safe

Panic

Rapid, erratic dispersal of the crowd. Individuals moving quickly in unpredictable directions, indicating a fear or emergency response.

High speed Erratic paths Mass dispersal
Critical

Aggressive

Hostile interactions within the crowd. Dense clustering, confrontational body language, and localized conflicts between individuals.

Dense clusters Confrontation Localized conflict
Danger

Idle

Stationary crowd with minimal movement. Individuals standing or sitting in place, typical of queuing, waiting, or gathering scenarios.

Low motion Stationary Grouped

Pipeline

How It Works

Three stages transform raw drone footage into an actionable crowd behavior prediction in seconds.

01
Frame Extraction

OpenCV decodes the uploaded video and extracts every frame, resizing each one to 112 × 112 px and normalising pixel values to [0, 1].

OpenCV NumPy
02
CNN-LSTM Inference

A sliding window of 16 frames is fed into the MobileNetV2 CNN backbone, whose spatial features are then processed by an LSTM to capture temporal dynamics.

MobileNetV2 LSTM TensorFlow
03
Majority Vote

Predictions from all sliding windows are aggregated via majority vote. The most frequent class is returned as the dominant crowd behavior.

Aggregation Flask API
